# Tutorial: Creating a Blinking LED Circuit

In this tutorial, we will create a classic Astable Multivibrator circuit.

## Step 1: Schematic Design
1.  Create a new project `Blinker.prjxml`.
2.  Place two NPN transistors and two LEDs from the library.
3.  Wire the components as shown below.
    <!-- > ![Schematic Diagram](./assets/tutorial-schematic.png) -->

## Step 2: PCB Layout
1.  Switch to PCB view.
2.  Arrange components to minimize crossing wires (Ratsnest).
3.  Route the tracks on the Bottom Layer.

## Step 3: 3D Visualization
1.  Assign 3D models to your LED and Transistor footprints.
2.  Open the 3D Viewer to inspect the final assembly.
